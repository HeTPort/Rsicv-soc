Contact **Hetport@outlook.com** and include:

- the person or organization requesting permission;
- the files, revision, and intended product or service;
- whether the target is simulation, FPGA, ASIC/IP, teaching, or manufacture;
- expected distribution, volume, support, and exclusivity needs; and
- any requested patent, trademark, warranty, or safety terms.

Sending an inquiry does not itself grant permission. Commercial rights begin
only after both parties enter a written agreement. Public notices may identify
the rights holder as `HeTPort`; a commercial agreement may require the legal
name or company identity of the contracting rights holder.
